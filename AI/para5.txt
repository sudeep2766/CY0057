Fashion has changed a lot over the years, reflecting the culture, lifestyle, and ideas of people in different periods. In ancient times, people mainly wore clothes to protect themselves from the weather and their surroundings. As time passed, clothing also became a way to express social status, culture, and personal identity. Different regions developed their own styles based on their traditions, climate, and available materials. For example, people in India wore traditional clothing such as sarees, dhotis, and kurtas, while other parts of the world developed their own unique forms of dress.During the Middle Ages, clothing often showed a person's wealth and position in society. Later, fashion became more creative and diverse, especially during the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries. The Industrial Revolution played an important role in this change by making clothes easier and faster to produce. In the twentieth century, fashion trends changed rapidly due to movies, music, celebrities, and popular culture. Jeans, T-shirts, skirts, and modern dresses became common choices for people of different age groups.Today, fashion continues to evolve with the influence of social media, online shopping, and global trends. People can explore different styles from around the world and choose outfits that match their personalities. At the same time, traditional clothing remains important during festivals, weddings, and cultural celebrations. Recently, sustainable fashion has also gained attention, encouraging people to reuse clothes, choose eco-friendly materials, and reduce waste. Overall, the evolution of fashion shows how clothing has grown from a basic necessity into a form of creativity and self-expression. It continues to change with society, technology, and individual preferences, making fashion an important part of everyday life.
