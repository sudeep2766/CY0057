Industrialisation can actually be good for the environment when it is planned and managed properly. As industries grow, they encourage the development of cleaner technologies that can reduce pollution and protect natural resources. For example, industrialisation has helped improve renewable energy technologies such as solar panels, wind turbines, and electric vehicles. These technologies can reduce our dependence on fossil fuels and lower greenhouse gas emissions.

Industries have also developed better ways to recycle waste, treat wastewater, and purify water, which helps reduce environmental damage. Modern factories are becoming more efficient as well. Advanced machines can use less energy and produce less waste compared to older methods of production.

Industrialisation can also support better public transportation and energy-efficient infrastructure. This can reduce the number of private vehicles on the roads and help lower air pollution. At the same time, governments can introduce environmental regulations that require industries to control emissions, treat wastewater, and dispose of waste responsibly.

Although industrialisation has caused pollution and environmental problems in the past, cleaner technologies and better production methods are changing its impact. Therefore, responsible industrialisation can help protect the environment by encouraging innovation, reducing waste, and using resources more efficiently. In this way, economic growth and environmental protection can go hand in hand.
